## Current policy and product rules

Apple section 1.2 calls for filtering objectionable posts, reports with timely responses, blocking and public contact details. Google requires accepted terms before creating/uploading UGC and effective ongoing moderation, with reporting/blocking appropriate to the experience. Neither requirement is replaced by an organizer verification badge or a campaign's financial approval threshold. Sources rechecked: [Apple App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#user-generated-content), [Google UGC policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9876937?hl=en-GB).

The user-defined rule remains: goals above GHS 250,000 require staff financial/publication approval unless the organizer is currently verified and has a prior published campaign. A safety hold must remain independent: financial eligibility cannot waive objectionable-content review. Lower goals continue to use their configured campaign tier policy.

## Implementation and verification requirements

- Use authoritative service-level admission before persistence/public side effects, not only browser checks or a URL regex. Validate the full proposed public version after merging edits. An admission decision must bind actor, resource/action and content fingerprint so edits or another author cannot reuse approval.
- Hold flagged submissions privately for staff review; distinguish held, rejected and unavailable screening from approved. Provider failure must not silently publish. Keep clear user feedback and a way to revise/appeal. Do not automatically replay a payment because its optional message was later approved.
- Inspect only necessary public content. Define and disclose the actual processor/data flow; existing per-request AI-writing permission does not authorize unrelated private-data sharing. Confirm provider terms and handling of sensitive fundraising stories with the privacy owner.
- Trace all projections: campaign pages, public profiles, comments, updates, supporter lists, realtime/outbox payloads, overlays, emails and existing caches. A hidden source record is insufficient if another projection still republishes its content.
- Test clean/flagged/unavailable/malformed screening, create/edit races, stale approval replay, different actors, staff decisions, deletion/retention and every public projection. Verify both web and native feedback. Language/media coverage and staffed response remain external evidence, not guarantees from mocked provider tests.

Current campaign creation and vanity URL admission are implemented and verified; see `PUBLICATION_REVIEWS.md`. Staff decision evidence is implemented in `CAMPAIGN_STAFF_REVIEW.md`. Next engineering slice: profile/payment attribution and media. Do not mark C09 complete after covering only AI-generated text or a single publishing surface.

## Campaign public-read boundary — implemented

The earlier source audit found that campaign financial-review states did not constrain public list/detail/share reads. This is now corrected: the shared public-state boundary permits only `active`, `funded` and `expired`. Draft, pending-review and blocked records are excluded before public list pagination/counts, and guest/unrelated-user detail reads return 404. Current owners and administrators retain authenticated review access. Slug/legacy-ID share previews, organization projections, related discussion/update/donation/collaborator reads, recent donations and live-session discovery/overlays enforce publication visibility. Public SSE checks visibility before replay/delivery and on heartbeats.

Web/native viewer-scoped caches clear on account/campaign changes and denied reloads. Web share/checkout pages clear their content and SEO state on denied focus refreshes. See `CAMPAIGN_VISIBILITY.md` for acceptance evidence and limits. This does not delete campaign records or alter financial balances.

`ReviewCampaignUseCase` and the moderation controller now bind decisions to the current reviewed version and administrator, with atomic status/audit/history persistence and explicit complete-content/fundraising review. See `CAMPAIGN_STAFF_REVIEW.md`. New campaign creation and URL changes now have a separate admission boundary. Legacy campaign approval now captures its own content/version evidence; closing reads alone does not screen old records.
