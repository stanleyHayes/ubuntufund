# Product naming and domain shortlist

Checked: 2026-08-10. This is an early collision/domain screen, not legal clearance. Before renaming, run Ghana Registrar-General and target-market trademark searches, reserve matching social handles, and purchase the chosen domains through the owner's registrar account.

## Why Ubuntu Fund should change

`ubuntu-fund.com` is already used by an active fundraising organization, and an Android product has also used “Ubuntu Fund”. The name therefore carries avoidable search, trademark, App Store, and user-trust confusion.

## Recommended shortlist

| Rank | Name | Positioning | Live `.com` RDAP check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **TumiRaise** | “Tumi” evokes strength/power in Akan; clear fundraising action; easy to pronounce internationally. | `tumiraise.com` returned no Verisign registration record |
| 2 | **SikaSpring** | A memorable Ghana-rooted image of money/resources beginning to flow; suitable beyond charitable giving. | `sikaspring.com` returned no Verisign registration record |
| 3 | **AbusuaRise** | Community and family progress; warm fit for personal, organization, and collective campaigns. | `abusuarise.com` returned no Verisign registration record |
| 4 | **BoaBridge** | “Boa” signals help/support while “Bridge” communicates connecting donors and causes. | `boabridge.com` returned no Verisign registration record |
| 5 | **Nkabomly** | Invented, compact form built around togetherness/unity; strongest chance of a defensible coined mark. | `nkabomly.com` returned no Verisign registration record |

## Recommendation

Proceed to legal and audience testing with **TumiRaise** and **SikaSpring**. TumiRaise is the clearest product name; SikaSpring is the more distinctive brand world. Reserve the `.com`, `.org`, `.africa`, and Ghana-specific domain variants together once the final name clears legal review.

Avoid **Ubuntu Fund**, **Boame**, **Nkabom Fund**, and **Obra Fund**: current search results show active organizations, projects, or financial products using those names or close variants.

Domain results are point-in-time only; an unregistered RDAP response is not a purchase guarantee.
